# TripWise AI — 3–5 Minute Faculty Presentation & Live Demo Guide

This guide walks through a structured 3–5 minute live demonstration of **TripWise AI** for faculty evaluation.

---

## Minute 1: Problem Statement & Evidence Traceability Preview (Landing Page)
1. Open **`http://localhost:5173`**.
2. Point out the core principle in the hero section: **Zero-Fabrication Travel Planning & Policy Assistant**.
3. Highlight the **Evidence Traceability Preview** card on the right:
   - Show the exact citation format: `"Check-in begins at 3:00 PM."` → **Source: Sample Accommodation Policy (Harbourview Budget Inn), page 2** → **Status: Demonstration document — not a real booking policy**.
   - Point out the **Automated Multi-Source Conflict Detection** box showing how the system detects a tariff discrepancy between the *Destination Guide* (`INR 500`, closes `5:00 PM`) and the *Municipal Circular* (`INR 650`, Friday close `4:00 PM`).

---

## Minute 2: The Seeded Demonstration Scenario & 6-Stage Workflow Tracker (Dashboard)
1. Click **"Open Seeded Demo"** (or **"Dashboard"** in the top navigation bar).
2. Point out the seeded scenario header:
   - **Route:** `Mumbai, India → Sample City` (3 Days, 2 Adults, Total Budget `INR 25,000`, Cultural & Relaxed).
   - **Status Banner:** `Action Required — Documented Policy Conflict Detected (Not Fully Verified)`.
3. In **Tab 1 (Overview)**, show the **6-Stage Evidence & Policy Verification Tracker**:
   - Click on **Stage 2 (Knowledge Retrieval)** or **Stage 4 (Policy Validation)** to expand the live JSON output and stage warnings generated by the backend.
   - Click **"How this plan was created"** to display the 3-column explainability breakdown (Preferences Normalized, Curated Documents Consulted, and Validation Steps Performed).
   - Point out the **Budget Estimate & Breakdown** chart (`INR 17,496` estimated local ground cost = `70%` of the `INR 25,000` budget).

---

## Minute 3: Clickable Citations & Policy Validation Outcomes (Itinerary, Policy Checks & Missing Info)
1. Click **Tab 2 (2. Itinerary)**:
   - Show the Morning, Afternoon, and Evening schedule across Days 1, 2, and 3.
   - Click any green citation pill (e.g., `Sample Destination Travel Guide, page 1` or `Sample Accommodation Policy, page 2`).
   - Show the **Traceable Source Citation Modal** containing the document ID, page number, section heading, demonstration disclaimer, and exact text excerpt. Close the modal.
2. Click **Tab 4 (4. Policy Checks)**:
   - Use the filter buttons (`ALL`, `PASS`, `WARNING`, `FAIL`, `UNKNOWN`) to show that all four outcomes are genuinely produced:
     - **`PASS`**: Total Trip Budget Compliance (`INR 17,496 <= INR 25,000`) & Adult Guest Age/ID rules.
     - **`FAIL`**: Early Check-In & Pre-10:00 AM Lobby Luggage Drop-Off Restriction (traveller requested `8:30 AM` drop-off, conflicting with Page 2 of the PDF hotel policy).
     - **`WARNING`**: Public Transit Peak-Hour Baggage Rule (`15 kg` cabin bag limit) AND **Conflicting Source Evidence** (side-by-side comparison of the `INR 500` vs `INR 650` museum tariff).
     - **`UNKNOWN`**: Live Metro Departure Headways & Real-Time Opening Status.
3. Click **Tab 5 (5. Missing Info)**:
   - Show how Stage 5 flags:
     - **`Document date unknown`**: *Harbourview Budget Inn Policy* has `publication_date: null`.
     - **`Requires official confirmation`**: *Regional Entry Guidance* was published on `2024-06-01` (outdated).
     - **`Missing from knowledge base`**: Live transit schedules & intercity Mumbai–Sample City fares.
     - **`Current status not checked`**: Citadel Botanical Gardens North Watchtower seasonal closure & unconfigured Live Verification Adapter.

---

## Minute 4: Interactive Re-Planning & Resolving the Policy Conflict Live
1. Click the **"Revise & Re-Plan"** button in the top-right toolbar of the Dashboard.
2. Click the quick-fix button **"Quick Fix: Set Arrival to 3:30 PM (Resolves Policy FAIL)"** (or edit the budget/accommodation note manually) and click **"Save & Regenerate Itinerary"**.
3. Show that the backend re-runs all 6 stages immediately:
   - The `FAIL` count drops from `1` to `0`!
   - The accommodation check-in rule now evaluates to **`PASS`** with supporting evidence from Page 2.

---

## Minute 5: Document Library, Hybrid Search & Export
1. Click **Tab 6 (6. Document Library)**:
   - Run a live hybrid search query (e.g., click the quick query chip `check-in 3:00 PM early arrival luggage` or `National Museum admission fee Friday closing`) to show FAISS + BM25 retrieval scores and page citations.
   - Click **"Inspect Chunks"** on `Sample Accommodation Policy — Harbourview Budget Inn` (`PDF • 2 pages`) to show the extracted PyMuPDF page chunks.
2. Click **"Export .MD"** or **"Print / PDF"** in the top toolbar to demonstrate itinerary export.
